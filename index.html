Blessing for the Talit

For putting on a prayer shawl.
Read one word at a time, from top to bottom.

  1. בָּרוּךְ–Baruch
  2. אַתָּה–Atah
  3. יְיָ–Adonai
  4. אֱלֹהֵינוּ–Eloheinu
  5. מֶלֶךְ–Melech
  6. הָעוֹלָם–Ha’olam
  7. אֲשֶׁר–Asher
  8. קִדְּשָׁנוּ–Kid’shanu
  9. בְּמִצְוֹתָיו–B’mitzvotav
  10. וְצִוָּנוּ–V’tzivanu
  11. לְהִתְעַטֵּף–L’hitateif
  12. בַּצִּיצִית–Ba-tzitzit
יְיָ is read as Adonai.
